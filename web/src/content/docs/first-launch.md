The first time you open yapmod it looks and feels like Yapper, because it is Yapper — just in its own desktop window. A short welcome screen explains what yapmod adds, and then you are in.

## Signing in

If you are not signed in, yapmod opens the Yapper login screen. Sign in with your Yapper account and you land straight in the app. There is no separate yapmod account.

## Finding your way around

- **Your servers and channels** are all there, with unread and mention indicators so you can see what needs attention.
- **Notifications** reach you through the operating system, with sounds, so you can keep yapmod in the background.
- **Voice calls** work from the app. On macOS you will be asked to allow microphone access the first time.
- **External links** open in your normal browser instead of inside the window.
- **yapmod's settings** live in their own section inside Yapper's settings, next to everything else.

[Desktop integration](desktop-integration) goes through each of these; [What you get](features) is the quick tour.

## Keeping up to date

yapmod checks for updates when it starts and shows a notice if a newer version exists. You can turn the automatic check off in settings, and read the release notes for any version from the built-in changelog.
