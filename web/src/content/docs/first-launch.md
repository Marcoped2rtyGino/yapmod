## Signing in

If you aren’t signed in, yapmod opens the Yapper login screen. Sign in with your Yapper account and you land in the app.

## Links

External links open in your default browser instead of inside the yapmod window.

## yapmod settings

yapmod adds its own settings panel, organised in sections, separate from Yapper’s own settings. See [Features](features) for the full list.

## Keeping up to date

yapmod checks for updates and shows an update notice when a newer version exists. Download it from the Releases page and install over the old one.
